==========================
How a tester should think
==========================

What can I enter?
What should happen?
What shouldn't happen?
What happens at the boundaries?
What happens when the user does something unexpected?
What happens when the system/environment is unusual?

Bugs in the response: 
1. "Email is invalid" is a test scenario, not necessarily a test case.

For example, the scenario could be:
Verify that the system rejects an invalid email address.

2. The testing should be around the parameters along this is called "Boundary Testing"

E.g. for the case 8-20

7  ← just below minimum
8  ← minimum
9  ← just above minimum

19 ← just below maximum
20 ← maximum
21 ← just above maximum

3. This is a principle I want you to remember:
Test against requirements, not against what you personally think the application should do.
